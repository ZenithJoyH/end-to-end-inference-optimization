The six-doc no-logprob probe completed 24/24 requests with each endpoint internally exact on both repeats. Because the path change required a restart and the old full 8000 environment was not captured, **the 4/6 changes cannot be attributed to `LD_LIBRARY_PATH` alone**. This correction invalidates using accdiag-003's zero-code restart as an environment-identical control.

On the matched services, the remaining cross-port divergence IDs were `89,140,168`. Logprob requests on these three IDs exactly reproduced each service's no-logprob text and repeated exactly twice, so they are valid probes of those particular trajectories. Their first divergent tokens and top-two gaps were:

| Doc | First divergent token | 8000 selection and top-two margin | 8010 selection and top-two margin |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 89 | 4 | ` MC`, reported tie with ` chemistry` | ` chemistry`, ahead of ` MC` by 0.125 |

Thus all three sampled cross-port divergences are near-tied choices magnified into different reasoning trajectories. The 0.125-step margins are *consistent with*, but do not by themselves prove, finite-precision logit rounding. The local vLLM sampler converts incoming logits to float32 only immediately before sampling; this does not recover precision lost earlier in the BF16 model/LM-head path. No evidence here identifies a particular kernel, rank or model layer as erroneous.
